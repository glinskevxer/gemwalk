# The standards

Written by `gemwalk.tools.build_rules` out of `gemwalk/rules.py`. Do not edit:
change the table and run the tool.

The kinds of finding a reading can produce: 

- **temperature** — one of the temperature limits
- **oxygen** — one of the gas limits
- **nitrogen** — one of the gas limits
- **pressure** — one of the pressure limits
- **surface** — one of the surface limits

## Subpart WWW

New source performance standards for landfills built 1991 to 2016.

Cited as 40 CFR 60 subpart WWW.

| limit | value |
| --- | --- |
| wellhead temperature | 131.0 F (55 C) |
| wellhead oxygen | 5.0% |
| wellhead nitrogen | not available |
| wellhead gauge pressure | under +0.00" w.c. |
| surface methane | 500 ppmv above background |
| emission rate threshold | 50 Mg a year |
| design capacity threshold | 2,500,000 Mg |
| walkover pattern | 30 m serpentine |
| walkover wind | average under 5 mph, gusts under 10 mph |

| window | length |
| --- | --- |
| correct a wellhead finding | 5 calendar days |
| act on a wellhead finding | 15 calendar days |
| expand collection | 120 calendar days |
| repair a surface spot | 10 calendar days |
| remonitor a repaired spot | 10 calendar days from the repair |
| goes at the cover | 2 |
| collection in a filling area | 5 years |
| collection in a closed area | 2 years |
| system off for repair | 5 days |
| device off the run for a raise | 60 days |

Nitrogen may stand in for oxygen: no.

A higher operating temperature may be approved: yes.

An alternative to the pressure standard may be approved: no.
