# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- The wellfield: sites, filling areas with their collection deadlines, the
  devices sunk into the refuse, and the pipe tree between the wellheads and the
  blower.
- Monthly monitoring runs, read against whichever of the three federal
  standards the site is subject to, with the nitrogen alternative and an
  approved higher operating temperature both honoured per site and per device.
- The exceedance clock: five days, fifteen days, a hundred and twenty, all
  counted from the reading that opened it and never restarted by a recheck.
- Quarterly walkovers of the cover, with the wind and pattern conditions that
  throw a walk out, and the surface clock whose second window is counted from
  the repair rather than the reading.
- The blower station: control devices, the operating record, three hour block
  averages cut on the site's own clock, and outages.
- Liquid levels worked out from the tape drop, condensate sumps and haulage.
- Work orders that carry the deadline of the record they came out of.
- The emission rate at all three tiers.
- A command line over the whole of it, and the monthly and quarterly reports.
